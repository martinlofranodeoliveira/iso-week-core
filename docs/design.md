# Design — iso-week-core

Thursday anchors the ISO year; week 1 Monday derives from Jan 4.
Round-trips across Dec/Jan boundaries prove both directions agree —
the classic trap (Dec 29 belonging to next year's W01, Jan 1 to last
year's W53) is pinned by test, not by comment.

Oracle: 2026-W01 Thursday start, 2020-W53, year-boundary crossovers
both ways. Proleptic Gregorian only.
